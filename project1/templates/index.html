Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

Is this course really free? +
Yes! 100% free. No hidden charges, no credit card required. We believe education should be accessible to everyone. You only need internet and a device to join.
Is this beginner friendly? +
Absolutely! This course is designed for complete beginners. We start from zero. No prior coding knowledge needed. If you can use WhatsApp, you can learn coding with us.
In which language will classes be taught? +
Classes are taught in a mix of Hindi and English (Hinglish) - whatever makes concepts easier to understand. We adapt to what students are comfortable with.
What are the class timings? +
Live classes are 7:00 PM - 9:00 PM IST, 3 days a week (Mon, Wed, Fri). Perfect timing for students and working professionals. Recordings available if you miss a class.
Will I get a certificate? +
Yes! Upon completing the 8-week program and final project, you'll receive a certificate of completion that you can add to your LinkedIn and resume.
Are recordings available? +
Yes! All live classes are recorded and shared in the WhatsApp group. You can watch them anytime. Notes and resources are also provided after each class.
What if I have doubts after class? +
Our mentors are available 9 AM - 11 PM daily on WhatsApp for doubt resolution. You can also ask in the group - fellow students and mentors will help you out quickly.
Do I need a laptop or mobile is enough? +
Laptop is recommended for better coding experience, but you can start with mobile too. We'll guide you on setting up free online coding environments if needed.
